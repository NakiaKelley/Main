This premium wrap combines classic club sandwich flavors with convenient grab-and-go portability.

- Flexibility in ingredient sourcing while maintaining product consistency

**Sustainability Considerations:**

- **Shelf Life:** 3-5 days refrigerated at 38°F-40°F
- **Assembly Method:** Cold assembly only
- **Packaging:** Deli paper wrap + craft box or clamshell

## 5. PRODUCTION PROCEDURES

### 5.1 Smoky Mayo Preparation

**Time Required:** 10 minutes  
**Skill Level:** Basic  
**Training Focus:** Sauce preparation, seasoning balance, taste testing

**Procedure:**

1. **Gather Ingredients**
   - Hellmann's mayonnaise: 2 cups (16 oz)
   - Smoked paprika: 2 tsp
   - Garlic powder: 1 tsp
   - Black pepper: ½ tsp
   - Onion powder: ½ tsp
   - Salt: ¼ tsp

2. **Combine Ingredients**
   - Place mayonnaise in medium mixing bowl
   - Add all dry seasonings to mayonnaise
   - Whisk thoroughly until fully incorporated
   - Scrape sides and bottom of bowl to ensure even distribution
   - Mix should be uniform rust/coral color from smoked paprika

3. **Quality Check**
   - Texture: Smooth, no lumps or dry spice pockets
   - Color: Even rust/coral color throughout
   - Taste test: Balanced smoky, garlicky, savory flavor
   - Should not be overpowering in any single spice

4. **Adjust if Needed**
   - Too smoky: Add ¼ cup more mayo
   - Not enough flavor: Add small pinch of each seasoning, mix, taste again
   - Document any adjustments on batch log

5. **Store**
   - Transfer to squeeze bottle (for easy application) or storage container
   - Label: "Smoky Mayo - Date - Use By [Date + 7 days]"
   - Refrigerate at 38°F-41°F
   - Shelf life: 7 days

**Critical Control Point:**
- Mayo must remain at ≤41°F throughout preparation and storage
- Discard if mayo is left at room temperature >2 hours

**Training Notes:**
- Teach importance of thorough mixing for even flavor distribution
- Practice taste testing and seasoning adjustment
- Discuss how smoked paprika provides both color and flavor
- Emphasize proper labeling with dates

**Yield:** Approximately 16 oz (enough for 16 wraps at 1 oz per wrap)

---

### 5.2 Bacon Cooking

**Time Required:** 25 minutes (15-20 min cooking + 5-10 min cooling)  
**Skill Level:** Intermediate  
**Training Focus:** Protein cooking, temperature monitoring, food safety

**Procedure:**

1. **Preheat Oven or Griddle**
   - Oven method: Preheat to 400°F
   - Griddle method: Heat to medium-high (350°F-375°F)

2. **Prepare Bacon**
   - Line sheet pan with parchment paper or foil (oven method)
   - Separate bacon strips (12 strips total for batch)
   - Lay in single layer, not overlapping
   - Slightly stretching strips prevents curling

3. **Cook Bacon**
   - **Oven method:**
     - Place sheet pan in preheated 400°F oven
     - Cook 15-20 minutes until crispy
     - Check at 15 minutes, rotate pan if needed for even cooking
     - Continue until bacon is golden-brown and crispy
   
   - **Griddle method:**
     - Place strips on heated griddle
     - Cook 4-5 minutes per side
     - Flip once halfway through
     - Cook until golden-brown and crispy

4. **Verify Temperature**
   - Use food thermometer
   - **Critical Control Point:** Internal temperature must reach 145°F minimum
   - Document temperature on batch log

5. **Drain Bacon**
   - Remove from heat source
   - Transfer immediately to paper towel-lined plate
   - Allow excess grease to drain
   - Pat tops with additional paper towels if very greasy
   - Optional: Save bacon fat for other recipes (sustainability practice)

6. **Cool Bacon**
   - Let bacon cool at room temperature for 10-15 minutes
   - **CRITICAL:** Bacon must cool to ≤40°F before assembly
   - To speed cooling: Transfer to clean plate, refrigerate
   - Do not stack while still hot (prevents steaming/sogginess)

7. **Portion and Store**
   - Once cooled, portion: 2 strips per wrap
   - Store in covered container in refrigerator
   - Keep refrigerated at 38°F-40°F until assembly
   - Use within 24 hours of cooking for optimal quality

**Critical Control Points:**
- Cook bacon to 145°F minimum internal temperature (food safety)
- Cool bacon to ≤40°F before assembly (cold chain maintenance)
- Do not leave cooked bacon at room temperature >2 hours

**Training Notes:**
- Teach proper use of food thermometer
- Discuss importance of single layer for even cooking
- Explain "carry-over cooking" (bacon continues cooking briefly after removal from heat)
- Practice identifying visual doneness cues: golden-brown color, crispy texture
- Emphasize importance of cooling before assembly for food safety

**Quality Standards:**
- Crispy texture (not chewy or soft)
- Golden-brown color (not pale or burnt)
- Well-drained (not greasy)
- Flat (not excessively curled)

**Yield:** 12 cooked strips (approximately 8-9 oz cooked weight)

### 5.3 Turkey Breast Slicing

**Time Required:** 20 minutes  
**Skill Level:** Intermediate to Advanced  
**Training Focus:** Knife skills, uniform slicing, protein handling safety

**Procedure:**

1. **Prepare Work Area**
   - Use dedicated cutting board for cooked protein (color-coded if available)
   - Ensure chef's knife is sharp (dull knives are dangerous and produce ragged cuts)
   - Have scale ready for portioning
   - Prepare storage container for sliced turkey

2. **Retrieve Turkey Breast**
   - Remove cooked turkey breast from refrigeration
   - Verify temperature ≤40°F
   - Check for any off-odors or discoloration (discard if present)
   - Pat dry if excess moisture present

3. **Slicing Technique**
   - Place turkey breast on cutting board
   - Identify grain direction (muscle fiber lines)
   - **CRITICAL:** Slice AGAINST the grain for tender texture
   - Use smooth, long slicing motions (not sawing)
   - Target thickness: ⅛ inch slices
   - Hold knife at slight angle for clean cuts
   
4. **Maintain Consistency**
   - Practice uniform thickness throughout batch
   - Inconsistent thickness affects texture and appearance
   - Thin slices are tender; thick slices are chewy
   
5. **Portion Turkey**
   - Stack slices and weigh
   - Portion: 4 oz per wrap (6 portions total)
   - Place each portion in separate pile or container section
   - Cover to prevent drying

6. **Handle Scraps**
   - Small end pieces or irregular slices can be:
     - Diced for use in salads
     - Saved for staff meals
     - Used in other menu items (reduce waste)
   - Do not use dried out or discolored pieces

7. **Store**
   - Transfer portioned turkey to covered container
   - Label: "Sliced Turkey - Date - Use By [Today + 2 days]"
   - Refrigerate immediately at 38°F-40°F
   - Use within 2 days of slicing for optimal quality

**Critical Control Points:**
- Keep turkey at ≤40°F throughout slicing process
- Use clean, sanitized cutting board and knife
- Wear gloves when handling ready-to-eat turkey
- Minimize time at room temperature (<1 hour total)

**Training Notes:**
- Demonstrate proper knife grip and cutting motion
- Teach how to identify grain direction
- Practice maintaining uniform thickness
- Discuss proper knife safety and cut prevention
- Emphasize importance of sharp knives for safety and quality
- Show how to handle protein safely (gloves, clean equipment)

**Training Progression:**
- Beginner: Practice on sample pieces, focus on safety
- Intermediate: Work on consistency and speed
- Advanced: Perfect uniform slices, efficient portioning

**Quality Standards:**
- Uniform ⅛ inch thickness
- Clean, smooth cuts (not ragged or torn)
- Moist appearance (not dried out)
- Sliced against the grain

**Yield:** 24 oz sliced turkey (six 4 oz portions)

### 5.4 Vegetable Preparation

**Time Required:** 15 minutes  
**Skill Level:** Basic to Intermediate  
**Training Focus:** Knife skills, food safety with produce, thorough washing

**GRAPE TOMATOES**

**Procedure:**

1. **Wash Tomatoes**
   - Place grape tomatoes in colander
   - Rinse thoroughly under cold running water
   - Inspect for any soft spots, bruising, or mold
   - Remove and discard any substandard tomatoes

2. **Dry Tomatoes**
   - Shake colander gently to remove excess water
   - Pat dry with clean paper towels
   - Excess moisture can make wrap soggy

3. **Slice or Quarter Tomatoes**
   - Using sharp paring knife or chef's knife
   - Cut each tomato in half lengthwise
   - For larger grape tomatoes, can quarter
   - Cutting exposes flavor and makes easier to eat

4. **Portion Tomatoes**
   - Weigh or measure: 2 oz per wrap
   - 2 oz ≈ ¼ cup or 4-6 halved grape tomatoes
   - Place each portion in separate container or section
   - Cover and refrigerate until assembly

**ROMAINE LETTUCE**

**Procedure:**

1. **Inspect Lettuce**
   - Examine heads for freshness
   - Remove outer leaves if wilted, bruised, or damaged
   - Use inner leaves preferentially (more tender, less bitter)

2. **Wash Lettuce Thoroughly**
   - Fill large bowl or sink with cold water
   - Separate leaves and submerge
   - Agitate gently to remove dirt and debris
   - Lift leaves from water (leaves dirt settled at bottom)
   - Repeat if water is very dirty
   - **CRITICAL:** Thorough washing is essential for food safety

3. **Dry Lettuce Completely**
   - Use salad spinner for best results
   - Spin in batches until leaves are completely dry
   - Excess water dilutes ranch dressing and makes wrap soggy
   - If no salad spinner: Pat dry thoroughly with clean towels or paper towels
   - Allow to air dry for a few minutes if needed

4. **Chop or Tear Lettuce**
   - Remove thick stem/rib portions (can be composted)
   - Chop or tear leaves into bite-sized pieces (approximately 1-2 inch pieces)
   - Smaller pieces are easier to eat in wrap format

5. **Store Temporarily**
   - If not dressing immediately, refrigerate chopped lettuce
   - Cover with damp paper towel to prevent wilting
   - Do not dress until ready to use (dressed lettuce wilts quickly)

**Critical Control Points:**
- Wash all produce thoroughly (removes dirt, bacteria, possible pesticide residues)
- Dry lettuce completely (prevents sogginess, maintains dressing adhesion)
- Keep produce refrigerated at 38°F-41°F
- Use within 2 days of washing and prep

**Training Notes:**
- Demonstrate proper washing technique
- Teach importance of thoroughly drying lettuce
- Practice consistent knife cuts
- Discuss food rescue produce: same washing and quality standards apply
- Show how to identify quality produce (firmness, color, absence of decay)

**Quality Standards:**
- Tomatoes: Firm, bright red, no soft spots
- Lettuce: Crisp, vibrant green, no wilting or browning
- All produce clean and thoroughly washed
- Completely dry before use

**Yield:**
- Tomatoes: 12 oz prepared (six 2 oz portions)
- Lettuce: 24 oz prepared (six 4 oz portions)

### 5.5 Lettuce Dressing

**Time Required:** 5 minutes  
**Skill Level:** Basic  
**Training Focus:** Even distribution technique, portion consistency

**Procedure:**

1. **Prepare Work Area**
   - Use large mixing bowl
   - Have washed and dried lettuce ready
   - Measure ranch dressing

2. **Combine Lettuce and Ranch**
   - Place all prepared romaine lettuce in large bowl (24 oz total)
   - Measure ranch dressing: 12 oz (1½ cups) total
   - **Ratio:** 4 oz lettuce + 2 oz ranch per wrap
   
3. **Toss Thoroughly**
   - Using clean hands (gloved) or large tongs
   - Toss lettuce and ranch together
   - Ensure every leaf is coated evenly
   - Toss for 30-60 seconds minimum
   - Scrape sides of bowl to incorporate all dressing

4. **Quality Check**
   - All lettuce should be lightly coated
   - No dry leaves remaining
   - No pooling of excess dressing at bottom of bowl
   - If too dry: Add small amount of additional ranch, toss again
   - If too wet: Add more lettuce if available, or drain excess

5. **Portion and Store**
   - Portion immediately into six 4 oz portions (dressed lettuce)
   - Place in separate containers or assembly station
   - Cover and refrigerate at 38°F-40°F
   - **Use within 2 hours of dressing** (dressed lettuce wilts quickly)

**Critical Control Point:**
- Maintain cold chain: Lettuce must stay ≤41°F
- Assemble wraps soon after dressing lettuce (within 2 hours maximum)

**Training Notes:**
- Teach importance of even distribution
- Show how to assess proper dressing amount (lightly coated, not drenched)
- Discuss timing: dress lettuce close to assembly time
- Practice portioning by weight for consistency

**Quality Standards:**
- Lettuce evenly coated with ranch
- No dry leaves
- No excess pooling of dressing
- Still crisp (not wilted)

**Yield:** 24 oz dressed lettuce (six 4 oz portions)

### 6.2 Assembly Procedure (Cold Assembly Only)

**Time Required:** 20 minutes for 6 wraps  
**Skill Level:** Intermediate  
**Training Focus:** Assembly technique, wrap rolling, portion control

**Step-by-Step Assembly:**

1. **Prepare Assembly Line**
   - Lay out all 6 spinach wraps (12") on clean work surface
   - Have all components within easy reach
   - Position in order of layering for efficiency

2. **Apply Smoky Mayo**
   - Spread 1 oz smoky mayo in center of each wrap
   - Use spoon or squeeze bottle for application
   - Spread in 6-inch circle in center of wrap
   - Leave 2-inch border clear on all edges (prevents leaking)

3. **Layer Turkey**
   - Place 4 oz sliced turkey on smoky mayo
   - Arrange in center, keeping within mayo circle
   - Overlap slices slightly for even coverage
   - Do not extend to edges

4. **Add Bacon**
   - Place 2 strips cooked bacon on turkey
   - Can break into pieces if needed for better distribution
   - Lay flat across turkey

5. **Add Cheese**
   - Sprinkle 2 oz shredded cheddar evenly over bacon
   - Distribute across entire filling area
   - Cheese helps bind ingredients together

6. **Add Tomatoes**
   - Place 2 oz sliced grape tomatoes over cheese
   - Distribute evenly
   - Avoid concentrating in one area

7. **Add Dressed Lettuce**
   - Top with 4 oz dressed romaine lettuce
   - This goes on last (top layer)
   - Mound in center, keeping within border

8. **Assess Fullness**
   - Filling should be substantial but not overflowing
   - Should be able to see clear 2-inch border around filling
   - If overfilled, reduce portions slightly for remaining wraps

**Rolling Technique:**

9. **Fold Bottom**
   - Fold bottom edge of wrap up and over filling
   - Tuck edge under filling slightly
   - This creates bottom seal

10. **Fold Sides**
    - Fold left side in toward center
    - Fold right side in toward center
    - Sides should overlap slightly in center

11. **Roll Tightly**
    - Starting from bottom (already folded), roll wrap forward
    - Roll tightly to compress ingredients and prevent air pockets
    - Keep tension consistent as you roll
    - Finish with seam side down

12. **Assess Roll Quality**
    - Wrap should be tight, not loose
    - No gaps or air pockets
    - Filling visible at both ends
    - Wrap structure intact (no tears)

**Packaging:**

13. **Wrap in Deli Paper**
    - Place rolled wrap in center of deli paper (12" x 12")
    - Wrap tightly, folding edges in
    - Secure with tape if needed
    - Deli paper maintains shape and absorbs minor moisture

14. **Place in Box or Clamshell**
    - Insert wrapped sandwich into packaging
    - Ensure good fit (not too loose or too tight)
    - Package should protect wrap from damage

15. **Label Package**
    - Apply pre-printed label to package
    - Ensure label includes: Product name, ingredients, allergens, date, use-by date
    - Label should be clearly visible and securely attached

16. **Refrigerate Immediately**
    - Place packaged wraps in refrigerator
    - Store at 38°F-40°F
    - Refrigerate within 15 minutes of assembly completion (Critical Control Point)

## 8. FOOD SAFETY & HACCP

### 8.1 Critical Control Points (CCPs)

**CCP 1: Bacon Cooking Temperature**
- **Hazard:** Bacteria (Salmonella, Listeria) survival
- **Critical Limit:** Internal temperature ≥145°F
- **Monitoring:** Use calibrated food thermometer; check thickest part of bacon
- **Corrective Action:** If below 145°F, continue cooking until temperature reached
- **Verification:** Document temperature on batch log

**CCP 2: Component Cooling**
- **Hazard:** Bacterial growth in temperature danger zone (41°F-135°F)
- **Critical Limit:** All cooked components (bacon) must cool to ≤40°F before assembly
- **Monitoring:** Use food thermometer; verify bacon cooled before refrigeration
- **Corrective Action:** If not cooled, continue refrigerating until ≤40°F reached; do not use if left at room temperature >2 hours
- **Verification:** Document component temperatures on batch log

**CCP 3: Cold Chain Maintenance**
- **Hazard:** Bacterial growth during assembly and storage
- **Critical Limit:** All components must be ≤40°F during assembly; completed wraps must be refrigerated within 15 minutes
- **Monitoring:** Check temperatures before assembly; time assembly process
- **Corrective Action:** If assembly exceeds 15 minutes, immediately refrigerate completed wraps; if components exceed 40°F, do not use
- **Verification:** Temperature log and time stamps on batch log

**CCP 4: Storage Temperature**
- **Hazard:** Bacterial growth during storage period
- **Critical Limit:** Store wraps at 38°F-40°F
- **Monitoring:** Check refrigerator temperature twice daily
- **Corrective Action:** If temperature >40°F, immediately move product to functioning cooler; evaluate product safety (discard if >2 hours above 40°F)
- **Verification:** Refrigerator temperature log

### 8.2 Food Safety Procedures

**Personal Hygiene:**
- Wash hands thoroughly before starting production
- Wash hands after handling raw bacon, before handling ready-to-eat components
- Wear clean apron, hairnet, and gloves
- Change gloves between tasks (especially after handling raw protein)
- No jewelry, nail polish, or artificial nails

**Cross-Contamination Prevention:**
- Use separate cutting boards: raw bacon, cooked proteins, produce
- Sanitize work surfaces between preparation stages
- Use separate utensils for each component
- Never place cooked product on surface that held raw product (unless sanitized)
- Store raw bacon on lower shelves, ready-to-eat components on upper shelves

**Time and Temperature Control:**
- Maintain cold chain throughout production
- Minimize time components spend at room temperature
- Refrigerate components as soon as prepared
- Complete assembly within 15 minutes per batch
- Never leave product at room temperature >2 hours total

**Allergen Management:**
- Clearly label all products with allergen information
- Prevent cross-contact with other allergens
- Clean equipment thoroughly between products
- Train staff on allergen awareness

### 8.3 Sanitation Procedures

**Before Production:**
- Clean and sanitize all work surfaces with approved sanitizer
- Clean and sanitize all equipment and utensils
- Wash hands thoroughly

**During Production:**
- Sanitize work surfaces between raw and ready-to-eat tasks
- Change gloves frequently
- Discard any product that contacts unsanitized surface
- Wipe up spills immediately

**After Production:**
- Clean and sanitize all equipment and utensils
- Clean and sanitize work surfaces
- Empty and clean trash receptacles
- Sweep and mop floors

### 8.4 Product Dating and Storage

**Labeling Requirements:**
- Product name: "Turkey Club Wrap"
- Packed date: Date of assembly
- Use-by date: Packed date + 3 days (conservative) or + 5 days (optimal conditions)
- Storage instructions: "Keep refrigerated 38-40°F"

**Storage Guidelines:**
- Store at 38°F-40°F
- First in, first out (FIFO) rotation
- Do not stack excessively (can crush wraps)
- Keep away from raw products
- Monitor refrigerator temperature regularly

**Use-By Guidelines:**
- Conservative (3 days): Suitable for wholesale accounts with variable storage conditions
- Optimal (5 days): Suitable when consistent 38°F-40°F storage can be ensured
- Evaluate product quality before use-by date if storage conditions suboptimal
- Discard product that shows signs of spoilage (off-odors, discoloration, sliminess, mold)

## 11. TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

### 11.1 Common Issues and Solutions

| **Issue** | **Possible Cause** | **Solution** | **Prevention** |
|-----------|-------------------|--------------|----------------|
| Wrap tears during rolling | Tortilla too cold/stiff | Let wraps come to room temp 5-10 min before assembly | Store wraps properly; check expiration |
| Wrap tears during rolling | Overfilling | Reduce ingredient portions slightly | Practice consistent portioning |
| Ingredients fall out | Loose rolling | Practice tighter rolling technique; ensure filling is centered | Training on rolling technique |
| Wrap becomes soggy | Excess moisture from lettuce | Dry lettuce thoroughly after washing | Use salad spinner; pat dry with towels |
| Wrap becomes soggy | Too much ranch dressing | Reduce ranch slightly; ensure even distribution | Measure dressing accurately |
| Wrap becomes soggy | Tomatoes too juicy | Choose firmer tomatoes; remove excess moisture | Inspect tomatoes carefully; pat dry if needed |
| Bacon not crispy | Undercooked | Cook longer; verify 145°F temp | Monitor cooking time and temp carefully |
| Bacon not crispy | Overlapping on pan | Lay bacon in single layer | Use multiple pans if needed |
| Bacon too greasy | Insufficient draining | Drain longer on paper towels | Pat tops of bacon with additional towels |
| Turkey slices too thick | Knife technique | Practice smooth slicing motion; use sharp knife | Sharpen knives regularly; training |
| Turkey slices uneven | Inconsistent knife angle | Maintain consistent angle throughout | Practice; visual consistency checks |
| Smoky mayo too spicy | Too much smoked paprika | Reduce paprika to 1½ tsp; increase mayo to balance | Follow recipe precisely; taste test |
| Smoky mayo too strong | Too much garlic powder | Reduce garlic powder to ½ tsp; increase mayo | Follow recipe precisely; taste test |
| Lettuce wilted | Dressed too far in advance | Dress lettuce within 2 hours of assembly | Plan production timing accordingly |
| Lettuce wilted | Not dried properly | Dry lettuce thoroughly before dressing | Use salad spinner; allow drying time |
| Wraps not cold enough at assembly | Components not cooled properly | Verify all components ≤40°F before assembly | Monitor cooling time; use thermometer |
| Wraps not cold enough at assembly | Assembly taking too long | Work faster; assemble in smaller batches | Practice; streamline assembly line |

### 11.2 Quality Recovery

**If wrap tears during assembly:**
- Carefully salvage ingredients
- Use fresh wrap and reassemble
- Do not serve damaged wrap to customers

**If ingredients seem compromised:**
- Evaluate cause (temperature abuse, contamination, age)
- When in doubt, throw it out
- Do not serve product that appears unsafe

**If batch runs over time:**
- Document time on batch log
- Evaluate product safety
- If any component was at room temperature >2 hours total, discard

- May need multiple sheet pans for bacon
- Larger bowls for lettuce dressing
- Multiple mixing bowls for component staging

**Staffing:**
- Consider 2-person production for batches >12 units

- Small events or specialty orders
- Quality control testing

### 13.3 Batch Production Schedule

**For 300-400 Units Weekly Capacity:**

Assuming average production of 350 units/week (58 units/day, 6 days):
- Daily production: ~10 batches of 6 units each
- Production time per batch: 1.75 hours
- Total daily production time: ~17.5 hours (requires multiple shifts or staff)

**Recommended Approach:**
- Prep Day (Day 1): Prepare all components (mayo, bacon, turkey, vegetables)
- Assembly Day (Day 2): Assemble all wraps
- OR: Continuous production with overlapping prep and assembly

**Staffing for High Volume:**
- 2-3 staff members minimum
- Division of labor:
  - Staff A: Protein prep (turkey slicing, bacon cooking)
  - Staff B: Vegetable prep and dressing
  - Staff C: Assembly and packaging
- Rotate roles for skill development and reduce fatigue
