--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SOURCE RECIPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Full production specs, yields, and prep steps:
  recipe_card_roasted_vegetables.txt

Key components:
  - 1 1/2 lb Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved
  - 1 small butternut squash, peeled and cubed
  - 1 large red onion, peeled and cut into wedges
  - Olive oil, salt, pepper, 1 tsp chili powder
  - 1/3 cup pomegranate molasses (finishing glaze)
  - Pomegranate seeds (garnish)

Roast at 400 degrees F for 25-30 minutes, glaze with pomegranate molasses,
return to oven 3-5 minutes, finish with fresh pomegranate seeds.
