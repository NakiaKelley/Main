## 1. Executive Summary

The grab-and-go prepared foods market is experiencing rapid growth driven by several converging trends: protein-forward eating influenced by GLP-1 medications, fiber-rich diets, plant-based demand, global flavor profiles in portable formats, and consumer willingness to pay premium prices for fresh, healthy convenience food. The upcycled food market alone reached $66.8 billion in 2025 and is projected to reach $125 billion by 2035.

For Citizens Coffee & Catering, the intersection of food rescue and premium grab-and-go represents a unique market position — turning surplus ingredients into high-quality, health-forward prepared meals at accessible price points. This report analyzes the competitive landscape and identifies specific product opportunities for menu expansion.

## 2. Food Rescue & Upcycled Food Models

### 2.1 Commissary Kitchen Models Converting Surplus to Meals

| Organization | Location | Scale | Model |
|---|---|---|---|
| **DC Central Kitchen** | Washington, DC | 17,000 meals/day | Gold standard — rescues surplus, commissary production, culinary job training. 55-60% revenue from social enterprise contracts (school meals, cafes) |
| **Rethink Food** | New York City | 19,000+ meals/week | Partners with Whole Foods, JP Morgan dining, Chelsea Market for surplus donations. Commissary team creates meals for distribution |
| **L.A. Kitchen** | Los Angeles | 145,959 meals/year | "Strong Food" social enterprise — fresh meals for nonprofits, healthcare, universities. 223,260 lbs produce reclaimed annually |
| **Street Food Institute** | Albuquerque, NM | Storefront sales | Nonprofit commissary producing grab-and-go sold from storefronts, accepts SNAP/EBT |
| **Kitchens for Good** | San Diego | Community scale | Rescued food → prepared meals + culinary training |
| **Food Shift Kitchen** | Alameda, CA | Community scale | Surplus food → catering and prepared meals |

**Key takeaway:** DC Central Kitchen's model (rescue → produce → train → sell through social enterprise) is the proven playbook. 60+ kitchens have replicated this nationwide. Citizens Coffee is already operating this model — the opportunity is to scale and formalize it.

## 3. Major Healthy Grab-and-Go Brands

### 3.1 Farmer's Fridge
- **Scale:** 3,000+ vending machine locations in 20 states (airports, hospitals, offices, universities)
- **Projected:** 10 million meals sold in 2025; $100M+ raised
- **Format:** Mason-jar-style clear containers, machine-dispensed
- **Menu:** Salads ($9.99), bowls ($7.99), wraps, breakfast items, snacks
- **Shelf life:** ~1 week refrigerated
- **Surplus model:** Unsold meals donated to Feeding America
- **Key insight:** Proves that fresh grab-and-go in clear containers at ~$8-10 price points works at massive scale

### 3.2 Daily Harvest
- **Format:** Frozen, plant-based meals delivered to consumers
- **Products:** Smoothies, harvest bowls, flatbreads, soups, bites, lattes, oat bowls
- **Philosophy:** Whole fruits and vegetables, no preservatives, organic ingredients
- **Key insight:** Subscription model with single-serve frozen items

### 3.3 Snap Kitchen
- **Format:** Refrigerated prepared meals, physical retail locations + delivery
- **Products:** Macro-balanced bowls, proteins, sides, snacks
- **Philosophy:** Dietitian-designed, calorie/macro-labeled, fitness-oriented
- **Key insight:** Nutrition-forward labeling drives purchases for health-conscious consumers

### 3.4 Territory Foods
- **Format:** Fresh chef-prepared meals, delivered
- **Products:** Bowls, plates, breakfast items tailored to dietary preferences
- **Philosophy:** Local chef network, customizable dietary filters (keto, paleo, Whole30, etc.)

### 3.5 Simply Good Jars
- **Format:** Fresh layered salads in reusable mason jars
- **Backed by:** Shark Tank investment from Mark Cuban and Lori Greiner
- **Key insight:** The jar format provides natural tamper evidence, visual appeal, and batch production efficiency

## 4. 2025-2026 Grab-and-Go Industry Trends

### 4.1 Macro Trends Driving the Market

**Protein-forward everything** — The #1 trend. Driven by GLP-1 medication adoption (Ozempic, Wegovy), 45%+ of consumers prioritize protein. Products like Chipotle's High Protein Cup (32g protein/4oz) and Subway's Protein Pockets ($3.99, 20g+ protein) are bestsellers.

**Fiber is the new protein** — Whole Foods named fiber a top 2026 trend. 64% of Americans are intentionally consuming more fiber. Expect to see fiber content prominently labeled on packaging.

**Snacking replaces meals** — 58% of diners say grabbing a quick snack is a key reason for restaurant visits. Snacking is the fastest-growing restaurant daypart. 63% of breakfast eaters aged 18-34 prefer snacking over a full breakfast.

**GLP-1-influenced formats** — Protein-dense, smaller portion-sized, nutrient-rich items designed for consumers on weight management medications who eat less but need higher nutritional density.

**Sweet-savory combinations** — Miso caramel, tahini desserts, balsamic-cheese pairings are replacing 2025's "sweet-heat" trend.

**Global flavors in portable formats** — Middle Eastern (harissa, za'atar, sumac), Korean-inspired, Mediterranean, and Southeast Asian flavors in grab-and-go formats.

### 4.2 Consumer Behavior Shifts

- 24% of consumers now buy pre-made meals at grocery stores more often (QSR Magazine 2025)
- 28% of shoppers buy deli-prepared foods instead of restaurants (doubled from 2017)
- 66% of retailers say grab-and-go products are the best way to compete for prepared food sales
- 71% of urban office workers purchase breakfast during their commute (up from 52% a decade ago)
- Gen Z: 60% prefer snacking to meals; demand single-serve, Instagram-worthy, transparent packaging

### 4.3 Format Trends

| Format | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mason jar salads | **Resurgent** | 5-7 day shelf life, visual appeal, batch-friendly |
| Protein/snack boxes | **Growing fast** | Charcuterie-style, bento boxes, DIY snack kits |
| Overnight oats | **Maturing** | Now a standard offering, differentiate with toppings |
| Energy bites/balls | **Growing** | Low cost to produce, shelf-stable, high margin |
| Smoothie bowls | **Steady** | Requires immediate consumption, harder for grab-and-go |
| Heat-and-eat bowls | **Growing** | Microwave-safe packaging, extended shelf life |
| Veggie + dip cups | **Growing** | Simple, healthy, low cost |
| Grain bowls | **Dominant** | The #1 grab-and-go lunch format nationally |

## 5. Erewhon Market — Fresh Foods Case Study

*Erewhon is the premier upscale organic grocery chain in Los Angeles, known for celebrity collaborations, extreme-premium pricing, and trend-setting health food. While their price points are far above what Citizens Coffee targets, their product innovation and category mix is highly instructive.*

### 5.1 Philosophy & Positioning
- Organic, non-GMO, no seed oils, no refined sugars, no artificial ingredients
- Celebrity partnerships drive viral products (Hailey Bieber Strawberry Glaze Skin Smoothie was a cultural moment)
- In-house tonic bar, juice bar, hot bar, and prepared foods kitchen
- Target customer: health-conscious, high-income, trend-forward Los Angeles consumers

### 5.2 Smoothie Program (Their Signature Category)
Erewhon's smoothie collaborations are their most famous products, typically $19-$23 each:

| Smoothie | Celebrity/Brand Collab | Key Ingredients |
|---|---|---|
| Strawberry Glaze Skin | Hailey Bieber | Strawberry, dates, avocado, collagen, hyaluronic acid |
| Coconut Cloud | Wildly Organic | Coconut, vanilla, MCT oil, ashwagandha |
| Activated | Bella Hadid / Kin Euphorics | Adaptogens, mushroom blend, cacao |
| Acai Royale | Various | Acai, pitaya, blue spirulina, nut butter |
| Green Machine | House | Kale, spinach, banana, spirulina, protein |
| Matcha Latte | Various | Ceremonial grade matcha, oat milk, vanilla |

**Key insight for Citizens Coffee:** While $19 smoothies aren't the model, the *concept* of rotating signature drinks with story-driven ingredients is applicable at any price point. Consider a "Surplus Spotlight Smoothie" that changes weekly based on rescued ingredients.

**Salads:**

### 5.4 What Citizens Coffee Can Learn from Erewhon

1. **Category breadth matters** — Erewhon covers breakfast, lunch, snacks, drinks, and dessert with grab-and-go. Citizens Coffee can expand beyond meals into snack cups, energy bites, and wellness shots.
2. **Storytelling sells** — Every Erewhon product has a narrative (organic sourcing, celebrity collab, functional ingredients). Citizens Coffee's food rescue story is equally compelling and authentic.
3. **Visual presentation** — Clear containers, colorful layering, and Instagram-worthy appearance drive purchases. This is achievable at any price point.
4. **Functional ingredients** — Adaptogens, collagen, turmeric, and other functional ingredients command premium pricing. Consider adding a few functional items to the lineup.
5. **Rotating specials** — Erewhon constantly introduces new collaborations and limited-time items. Citizens Coffee naturally does this with surplus-driven weekly menus.

## 7. Packaging & Presentation Trends

### 7.1 What's Working in 2025-2026

- **Clear containers are king** — Consumers want to see what they're buying. Transparency = trust.
- **Tamper-evident packaging** — Shifted from nice-to-have to consumer expectation
- **Multi-functional** — Packaging that goes from fridge to microwave to table
- **Sustainability** — Recyclable/compostable materials, but not at the expense of food safety
- **Mason jar format** — Reusable, visual appeal, natural tamper evidence, 5-7 day shelf life for properly layered salads
- **Portion cups with lids** — 2-4 oz cups for dressings, dips, sauces on the side

### 7.2 Layered Jar Salad Method (Extended Shelf Life)

Properly layered jars can achieve 5-7 day refrigerated shelf life:

1. **Bottom:** Dressing / liquid
2. **Layer 2:** Hardy vegetables (carrots, beets, onions, beans)
3. **Layer 3:** Grains / pasta / softer vegetables
4. **Layer 4:** Protein (chicken, tofu, eggs)
5. **Layer 5:** Cheese, seeds, nuts (dry buffer)
6. **Top:** Lettuce / greens

**Key:** The dressing on the bottom never touches the greens on top until the consumer shakes/pours to eat.

### 7.3 Labeling Trends

- **Macro labels** — Calories, protein, fiber prominently displayed
- **Allergen icons** — Visual symbols, not just fine print
- **Shelf life / freshness dates** — Clear "Enjoy By" dates
- **Ingredient story** — Brief note about sourcing or mission ("Made with rescued surplus ingredients")
- **Dietary tags** — Vegan, GF, High-Protein, Keto as visual badges

## 8. Texas-Specific Landscape

### 8.1 Texas Food Rescue Ecosystem

| Organization | Location | Innovation |
|---|---|---|
| **Houston Food Bank** | Houston | Largest in US by distribution. Keegan Kitchen produces prepared meals for kids |
| **San Antonio Food Bank** | San Antonio | Fresh Produce Rescue + Second Servings (restaurant surplus) + Hunters for the Hungry (game meat) |
| **Tarrant Area Food Bank** | Fort Worth | Community Kitchen + Mission Market (free grocery shopping) |
| **Central Texas Food Bank** | Austin | MealConnect technology for food rescue logistics |
| **H-E-B** | Statewide | Diverted 87M lbs to composting; converts surplus produce → in-store guacamole, croutons, etc. |
| **Keep Austin Fed** | Austin | Connects restaurant surplus to recipients weekly |
| **The Stewpot** | Dallas | Rescues prepared food from hotels, convention centers, corporate cafeterias |
| **Rescuing Leftover Cuisine** | Dallas | Partners with cafes, hotels, bakeries for surplus rescue |

### 8.2 H-E-B as a Model

H-E-B's in-store upcycling is the closest commercial model in Texas to what Citizens Coffee does:
- Surplus produce that meets quality standards but hasn't sold → made into store-brand products
- Examples: fresh guacamole from ripe avocados, croutons from day-old bread
- 87 million pounds diverted from landfill in 2025

### 8.3 Texas Legal Protections

Texas law protects food donors from civil and criminal liability for donating wholesome food — an important enabler for the surplus-to-meal model.

### 8.4 Texas Food Insecurity Context

- 18.4% food insecurity rate
- 1 in 7 Texans experience food insecurity
- This creates both the need and the market opportunity for accessible, nutritious grab-and-go meals
