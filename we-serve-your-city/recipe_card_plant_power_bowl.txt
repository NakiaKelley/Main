┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ PRODUCTION STEPS                                                             │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                              │
│ PREP (Complete 1 day prior to assembly):                                    │
│   1. Cook quinoa: Rinse thoroughly, cook in 1:1.5 ratio (quinoa:water),    │
│      fluff, cool completely on sheet pans in walk-in refrigerator.         │
│   2. Cook brown rice: Rinse, cook in 1:2 ratio, cool completely.           │
│   3. Cook black beans: If using dried, soak overnight, cook with cumin,    │
│      garlic, bay leaf until tender, cool completely.                        │
│   4. Prepare chickpeas: Drain, pat very dry, toss with olive oil, smoked   │
│      paprika, cumin, garlic powder, salt, pepper.                           │
│   5. Roast chickpeas: Spread on sheet pan, roast at 400°F for 25-30 min    │
│      until crispy, shaking pan every 10 min, cool completely.              │
│   6. Roast sweet potato: Peel, cube ½ inch, toss with olive oil, salt,    │
│      paprika, roast at 425°F for 25-30 min until tender & caramelized.     │
│   7. Roast Brussels sprouts: Halve, toss with olive oil & salt, roast at   │
│      400°F for 20-25 min until crispy and charred, cool.                    │
│   8. Massage kale: Remove thick stems, tear leaves, massage with olive     │
│      oil, lemon juice, pinch of salt until softened & darkened.            │
│   9. Quick-pickle red onions: Slice thinly, bring vinegar, water, sugar,   │
│      salt to boil, pour over onions, cool, refrigerate minimum 1 hour.     │
│  10. Make tahini-lemon dressing: Whisk tahini, lemon juice, garlic, water, │
│      maple syrup, salt until smooth & pourable, refrigerate.                │
│  11. Toast pepitas: Dry toast in skillet over medium heat 3-5 min until    │
│      puffed & golden, cool completely.                                      │
│                                                                              │
│ ASSEMBLY (Cold assembly on day of production):                              │
│  12. Verify all components are at proper temperature (38°F-41°F).          │
│  13. Dice avocados immediately before assembly, toss with lemon juice.     │
│  14. Drain pickled onions thoroughly.                                       │
│  15. Set up assembly station with all ingredients in reach.                 │
│  16. For each container:                                                    │
│      - Layer 1 (bottom): 3 oz quinoa + 3 oz brown rice (mixed)            │
│      - Layer 2: 1.5 oz massaged kale                                       │
│      - Layer 3: 2.5 oz seasoned black beans                                │
│      - Layer 4: 2.5 oz roasted chickpeas                                   │
│      - Layer 5: 3 oz roasted sweet potato                                  │
│      - Layer 6: 2.5 oz roasted Brussels sprouts                            │
│      - Layer 7: 2.5 oz diced avocado                                       │
│      - Layer 8: 1 oz pickled red onion                                     │
│      - Top with: 0.5 oz pepitas, 0.3 oz hemp seeds, 0.3 oz nooch          │
│  17. Seal containers immediately after assembly.                            │
│  18. Package 2 oz tahini-lemon dressing separately in portion cup.         │
│  19. Apply VEGAN label with product name, assembly date, use-by date.      │
│  20. Store at 38°F-41°F until delivery.                                     │
│                                                                              │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ FOOD SAFETY NOTES                                                            │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│   • COLD ASSEMBLY ONLY - Do not assemble with hot components                │
│   • Time in Temperature Danger Zone (41°F-135°F): MAX 2 hours cumulative    │
│   • Wash hands before assembly and after handling each component             │
│   • Use clean gloves throughout assembly process                             │
│   • Sanitize all work surfaces before and after production                   │
│   • Store finished products at 38°F-41°F immediately                         │
│   • Date all components - use FIFO (First In, First Out)                    │
│   • Beans and grains are high-risk foods - cool rapidly after cooking       │
│   • Avocado is high-risk for browning - dice immediately before assembly    │
│   • Verify NO cross-contamination with animal products (dedicated tools)    │
